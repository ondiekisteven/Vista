# User Guide

## Open the app

Launch Vista and wait for the main library view to appear. The left side shows your available songs, while the right side previews the selected song and its slides.

---

## Search and select a song

Use the search box to filter the song list by title or lyrics. Click a song to view the slide preview.

---

## Present a song

Once a song is selected, send it to the output window so the congregation can see it. The preview updates as you move through the slides.

---

## Move through slides

Use the following keys while the slide list has focus:

- Space or Page Down: next slide
- Backspace or Page Up: previous slide
- Home: go to the first slide
- End: go to the last slide

---

## Adjust the output look

Open the output settings to change the display size, background mode, and text size. This is useful when your projector, screen, or room lighting requires a stronger contrast.

---

## Keep it simple

Vista is meant to be reliable during a service. Keep your song library organized, test your output display beforehand, and use the preview window to verify the layout before going live.
