# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Vista free to use?

The project is distributed with an included license. Please review the repository license file for the exact terms before redistribution or commercial use.

---

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Vista is designed to work locally without requiring a cloud account or persistent internet connection during a service.

---

## Does it support a second display?

Yes. A second display is recommended for presenting lyrics while the operator controls the service from the main window.

---

## Do I need an account?

No. Vista does not require account registration to use the app.

---

## Can I change the text appearance?

Yes. The output settings let you adjust font size, background mode, and background color for better readability.

---

## Can I use it for a church service?

Yes. The app is built around a simple, dependable worship workflow for song presentation and lyric display.
